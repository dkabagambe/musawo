## 5. Approval workflow

Build one generic workflow: DRAFT, SUBMITTED, IN_REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED (with required comment), APPLIED. Notify approvers; show status to submitters; record every step in the audit log; no one approves their own submission. Default policy table (editable by the Director in settings):
| Item | Prepared by | Reviewed by | Final approver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admission | Admin | none | Principal |
| Academic programme create, modify, archive | Principal | none | Director |
| Course or subject, new or modified | Principal | none | Director |
| Examination results publication | Instructor submits marks, Admin processes | Principal | Director |
| Certificate issuance | Admin lists, Principal requests | none | Director |
| Tuition payment proof | Admin collects | Principal | Director |
| Fee structure change, waiver, refund | Principal | none | Director |
| Equipment or practical supplies request | Admin prepares | none | Principal if available, otherwise Director (either can approve) |
| Storage clearing at a new intake | Admin prepares | Principal confirms | Director notified |
| Role assignments for Director and Principal | none | none | Director (Principal assigns other roles) |
Everything else the Principal does applies immediately but stays visible to the Director in Audit Logs and Reports. Principal's "Approval Tracking" lists submissions awaiting the Director.

## 6. Admissions and students

Flow: student submits the Apply form, Admin picks the details and registers the student (registration number generated by course, as now), Principal approves the admission, then the student is emailed that an account has been created with a set-password link and becomes ACTIVE. Setting requireAdmissionApproval (default true). Admin uploads and manages student documents. Student profile: editable phone, address, photo, emergency contact, password; locked email, full name, registration number, programme.

## 7. Payments

Student pays tuition (bank or mobile money, system-generated reference number) and the Admin collects the proof of payment and records it as a claim; the Admin forwards it to the Principal; the Principal forwards it to the Director for approval. Admin can view all payments, prepare reconciliation and reports, but cannot edit, verify or delete a payment record. Keep the unique transaction ID rule, statement import, reconciliation and reversals. Access to content follows only approved payments (existing percentage rules). Students see My Fees with balances and receipts.

## 8. Live classes (Google Meet) additions

- Students join in LISTEN-ONLY status by default; "Request to speak" raises a hand; the instructor grants or revokes speaking per student. The platform tracks the status and prompts the instructor; microphone control itself is applied in Meet, so show clear host instructions.
- ONLY the instructor can upload to the class Presentation panel (slides or PDF, view-only for students, no download); the instructor can temporarily give one student presenter rights.
- The instructor ticks the available students; roll call shows student name, registration number and course unit; saved and downloadable (CSV, PDF) at the start, at the end, or after class.
- Recording: keep the current behaviour (instructor adds the recording link or file; students replay in-platform; "Recording pending" reminder).

## 9. Breakaway groups and discussion forum

In a live class, the instructor creates groups from the enrolled students (default size 5, so 30 students make 6 groups). Provide BOTH modes per class: random assignment (with reshuffle) and pick by name (drag and drop or select), plus moving students between groups. Each group gets: a name, members, an optional Meet link field, a group discussion thread, and group work with ONE submission per group (members listed, group mark with optional individual adjustment). Students see their group. Each class has a discussion forum (instructor and students; group threads for breakaway groups).

## 10. Storage and retention

Keep class recordings and uploaded notes for at least one semester (intake). When the next intake starts, a scheduled job lists the previous semester's platform-stored recordings and notes and clears them to free space: preview with sizes, Principal confirms (Director notified), 14 day trash before permanent deletion (setting), option to export before clearing. NEVER delete marks, attendance, results, certificates, payments or audit logs. External links (Meet or Drive) are only unlinked. Show a storage dashboard (used, total, per course) to Principal and Director with a warning at 80 percent.

## 11. Concurrency (main requirement)

The platform must run many classes at the same time without crashing: several instructors teaching simultaneously while most users are logged in. Targets (settings, document in docs/CAPACITY.md): 20 simultaneous live classes, 600 logged-in users, 400 concurrent exam sessions. Video is handled by Google Meet, so protect the application: stateless app, Redis for presence, rate limits and caching, connection pooling, database indexes, pagination, per-widget streaming, background jobs for email, SMS, recordings and cleanup, server-side exam clocks with auto-submit, graceful degradation if Redis or an external service is down, health endpoint. Add k6 load tests (login storm, 20 classes with roll calls and presence, 400 exam sessions) and fix what they reveal. Report results and recommended server size.
