## 🔍 Specific Grammar Fixes

### Before:
```
"Hybrid and online healthcare training with 70% hands-on practicals..."
"Hybrid and online learning, with practical skills training..."
"...trainer delivering hybrid and online instruction..."
```

### After:
```
"Hybrid healthcare training with 70% hands-on practicals..."
"Hybrid classes with hands-on practical skills training..."
"...trainer delivering hybrid instruction..."
```

**Change Summary:**
- ❌ Removed: "Hybrid and online" (redundant, ungrammatical)
- ✅ Added: "Hybrid" (concise, grammatically correct)

**Result:** Professional, consistent messaging throughout the entire website.
